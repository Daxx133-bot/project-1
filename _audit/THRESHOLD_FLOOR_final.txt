VERDICT
============================================================================================
  The purely multiplicative threshold is NOT SAFE at this scale. At least one
  service exceeds it between two fault-free windows, so the rule will report
  'degraded' for services nothing happened to. Any count built on it -- and
  ancestor_affected_count is the PRIMARY outcome -- is contaminated.

  The error-rate branch of the same rule already carries an absolute floor
  (+0.05 absolute). The latency branch needs the equivalent.
